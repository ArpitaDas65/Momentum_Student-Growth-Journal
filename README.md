
# 🚀 Momentum – Student Growth Journal

> **Study Better. Grow Every Day.**

Momentum is a modern, responsive **Student Growth Journal & Productivity Dashboard** designed to help students organise their academic life, track progress, build productive habits, and stay consistent throughout the semester.

The project combines the clean aesthetics of modern productivity platforms with an interactive student-focused experience.

---

* Progress indicators

## 💡 Why Momentum?

Students often use multiple tools to manage their academic life—one for notes, another for assignments, another for schedules, and another for tracking habits.

**Momentum brings these experiences together in one focused workspace.**

The goal is simple:

> **Plan your work. Track your progress. Build consistency. Keep moving forward.**
